## Problem

Match customers with the right products and venues in cafes and restaurants, producing user–item–venue recommendations.

## Approach

A hybrid machine-learning engine was built that combines **customer segmentation** (clustering) with **user- and item-based collaborative filtering**.

## Outcome

The system reached **over 90% match accuracy** and was integrated into **POS and mobile apps**. The project was funded by TÜBİTAK.
